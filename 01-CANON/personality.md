# Personality

## CANON: positive traits
1. **Charismatic:** socially magnetic, approaches people easily.
2. **Quick-witted:** spontaneous, irreverent, frequently profane.
3. **Perceptive:** visually observant and socially sharp, without guaranteed empathy.
4. **Passionate:** seriously committed to photography.
5. **Audacious:** fearless, risk-taking, hard to embarrass.

## CANON: negative traits
1. **Hedonistic:** prioritizes pleasure and intoxication.
2. **Exploitative:** benefits from social connections and other people's generosity.
3. **Volatile:** angry, sometimes deliberately cruel when provoked.
4. **Possessive:** jealousy and double standards.
5. **Self-destructive:** sabotages opportunities through impulsivity and substance use.

## WORKING CHARACTERIZATION
- Charming, sexually reckless, hedonistic, provocative and often very funny.
- **Ketamine addiction is established canon** and contributed, alongside toxic relationship behavior, to the breakdown of his engagement to Darla. Exact onset, severity, treatment, recovery status and specific incidents are unknown.
- Substance use follows cycles of heavy use and superficially stable intervals; depict consequences realistically. Do not assume recovery or constant intoxication.
- Storytelling tone is socially comic without denying adult consequences.
- Family discussed as loving but exhausted by his choices; particulars remain unresolved.
- Professional demeanor can be quiet and stoic despite a loud social life.


## CANON: superficial interests and shallow general knowledge
- Nikolai is deliberately characterized as **shallow, surface-level, and somewhat superficial**. He has many genuine interests but often knows surprisingly little about their history, meaning or surrounding context.
- His taste, social confidence and strongly expressed opinions can exceed his factual knowledge. He likes experiences, looks, sounds and cultural status without routinely researching them.
- **This is not an assertion that he is unintelligent, ignorant about everything, or pretending to enjoy what he likes.** The pleasure and aesthetic judgment can be sincere even when the intellectual engagement is thin.
- **Photography is the important exception:** his established passion and practical skill should remain credible. Technical competence can coexist with limited interest in theory, criticism or art history; precise limits remain to be developed.
- The specific examples of not knowing a band's nationality, bluffing about literature, superficial politics, and poorly understood travel are **PROPOSED illustrations**, not individually approved events or beliefs.


- **Sexual humor with male friends:** Nikolai regularly makes gay or homoerotic sexual jokes with his male friends as part of his irreverent, comfortable, provocative banter. This is a recurring mannerism, not a claim about any particular friend's sexuality, attraction, or response. Nikolai remains canonically bisexual.
